01 The problem

A dropped signal shouldn't be a lost sale.

In a busy market the network comes and goes all day. When it goes, the card machine stops, the transfer app spins, and the customer walks. T-Pay moves the one step that needs the internet, locking the money, to a moment when you have it, and keeps the payment itself between the two phones.

i.

Lock it while you can.

Put money in the Offline vault from PayPal or your Wallet whenever you have signal. It waits there, ready to spend, for up to 24 hours at a time.

ii.

Nothing moves without your fingerprint.

Every payment is signed by a key that never leaves your phone, and only after your fingerprint or PIN. The assistant can propose. It can't pay.

iii.

Signed on the phone. Settled by PayPal.

The seller's phone checks the payment on the spot. When either phone comes back online, T-Pay captures it from the PayPal hold and the money lands.

03 The system

Two wallets, one palette, and one rule about money.

The Offline vault spends with no network. The Wallet holds what you receive and add, and talks to PayPal. The rule: software can suggest, read out and prepare, but money moves only after a person's fingerprint, PIN or PayPal sign-in.

What keeps a payment honest

Checked on the phone, then again on the server
Device keyA key that never leaves the phoneEach phone makes its own P-256 key in secure storage and registers it once. Every payment is signed with it.
CertificatesTrust with no networkDevices and vaults carry certificates signed by T-Pay's root key. The app ships with the public half, so it can check them offline.
SequenceCounted the moment it's signedEach payment takes the next number under a vault that states its total. A reused number, or a total over the lock, is refused.
ConfirmationFingerprint or PIN, every timePaying, withdrawing by voice and closing a vault all stop for the person. The assistant never enters a PIN.

Status, in words and colour

Never colour alone
Queued

Moving: waiting for a network

Received and checked on the phone. A light circles it until a phone reconnects and T-Pay settles it.

Paid

Still: it's done

Captured from the PayPal hold and in the Wallet. Nothing more to wait for, so nothing moves.

Refused

Stopped: and it says why

Over the vault's limit, already received, or changed after signing. The screen names the reason in plain words.
